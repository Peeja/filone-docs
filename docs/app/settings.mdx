title: Settings
description: Profile fields, your organizations, notification preferences, and account security in the Fil One console.
---

**Settings** is in the user menu at the bottom of the left sidebar. It holds your profile, the organizations you belong to, your notification preferences, and your account security. Every member of an organization has their own Settings.

## Profile

| Field | Rules |
|-------|-------|
| **Full name** | Cannot be empty, up to 200 characters |
| **Email** | Must be a valid address. Disposable addresses are rejected |

**Save changes** stays disabled until you edit one of the fields, and a note beside it lists what will be saved.

:::warning Changing your email address sends you back through verification
Saving a new address marks the account unverified, sends a verification email to the new address, and drops you straight onto the verification screen. Every page redirects there until you confirm, exactly as for a new account.

You can edit the address again while unverified, so a typo is recoverable.
:::

**If you signed in with Google or GitHub, your name and email are managed there.** Both fields are read-only for social sign-ins, labelled *Managed by Google* or *Managed by GitHub* with a link straight to that provider's profile page, so you keep one source of truth for them.

Your organization's name is not part of your profile. Owners and Admins rename it on the **Edit organization** page, opened from the organization menu at the top of the sidebar, where the name must be 2 to 100 characters, using letters, numbers, spaces, hyphens, and periods only.

## Organizations

The **Organizations** section lists every organization you belong to, with your role and when you joined. Each row's menu offers **Switch to this organization** and **Leave organization**. Leaving removes your access to that organization and everything in it, and you can only rejoin with a new invitation. The last Owner of an organization can't leave it. See [Organization](/app/organization#leaving-an-organization).

## Notifications

**Marketing emails** ("Receive updates about new features") is a working toggle. It reflects your current subscription status, is off until you turn it on, and saves as soon as you flip it, with no **Save changes** button.

:::note Keep an eye on the trial caps
There is no notification setting for uploads or storage limits. Keep an eye on the usage cards on the [Dashboard](/app/dashboard), which show your storage and egress against the trial caps. This is worth doing during the trial, where reaching a cap changes what the account can do. See [Usage](/billing/usage).
:::

## Security

Two-factor authentication, passkeys, recovery codes, and password changes all live in Settings under **Security**. Password is managed by your provider on Google and GitHub accounts, so no **Change** button appears.

See [Account Security](/security/account-security) for the full walkthrough.

## Danger zone

The last card on the page is **Danger zone**, with **Delete account**. Deleting your account is not available in the console yet: the card asks you to email [support@filone.com](mailto:support@filone.com), and the team will take it from there.

Deleting an organization is separate, and is covered on the **Edit organization** page. See [Deleting an organization](/app/organization#deleting-an-organization).
